Quick help

Common questions about using STICHA.

?
What is STICHA?

STICHA is a digital measurement book for tailors. It keeps customer details, measurements, notes and delivery dates organised in one simple place.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. STICHA is designed to work on phones, tablets and desktop screens, with large touch-friendly controls on smaller screens.

Where are my customer records saved?

STICHA is offline-first. Records are saved locally so you can keep working without internet. Supabase can be connected later for secure account-based cloud sync.

Can I use inches and centimetres?

Yes. Choose Inches or Centimetres before entering measurements. Each customer record remembers its selected unit. You can also add custom measurement fields.

How do delivery reminders work?

Set a starting date and delivery date. Turn alarms on and STICHA checks due orders while the app is open and can show a browser notification if permission is granted.

Can I change the colours?

Yes. Open Appearance to choose any of the ten background themes or switch your text style.

Can I use my own ringtone file?

The current version provides ten built-in emergency-style tones. A future version can support uploaded audio files and deeper device-level notification settings.
